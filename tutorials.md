# Dishcision User Guide 🍽️

This tutorial walks you through planning the perfect group lunch or dinner using **DISHCISION** directly in your LINE Group Chat.

---

## 👥 Understanding User Roles

Before you start, here are the three roles involved in planning a dining event on DISHCISION:

| Role | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Organizer** | The person who creates the meeting, configures the decision weights, initiates tie-breaker games, and confirms the final dining booking. |
| **General Attendee** | Group members invited to join the meeting. They input their calendar schedules, preferred/avoided categories, and vote or veto suggested options. |
| **Key Person** | A highlighted attendee (e.g., the guest of honor, birthday celebrant, or group leader) whose preferences carry a significantly higher weight (between 70% and 90%) in recommendations. |

---

## 🟢 Step 0: Set Up DISHCISION in Your LINE Group
**👤 Involved Roles: All Members**

Before creating any meetings, everyone needs to be connected:

1. **Create a LINE group** — Open LINE, create a new group, and add the friends you want to dine with.
2. **Add DISHCISION bot to the group** — Invite DISHCISION into your group chat.
3. **Add DISHCISION as a friend** — Search for the DISHCISION official account in LINE or scan the QR code or Add from Group. Make sure every member adds DISHCISION as a friend first.
4. **All members send `#register`** — Each person types `#register` in the group. The bot will reply confirming your profile is linked.

---

## 🎬 Step 1: Create a Meeting 
**👤 Involved Role: Organizer**

As the organizer, you decide the basic boundaries of the gathering:

1. In your LINE group chat, type **`#create-meeting`** (or **`#สร้างนัด`**) and send it. Tap the **Create Meeting** button in the bot's reply.
2. Fill out the **Meeting Details**:
   - **Topic**: Give your event a fun name (e.g. *Friday Night Hangout* or *John Doe's Birthday Celebration*).
   - **Timing Mode**: 
     - Choose **Voting** if you want members to vote on dates/times. Select the start and end dates of the voting range, and the meal type (Lunch, Dinner, or Any).
     - Choose **Fixed Time** if the date and time are already set.
   - **Invite Friends**: Select the group members you want to join.
   - **Key Decision Makers**: Flag participants who should be a **Key Person** (such as the guest of honor).
   - **Key Decision Ratio**: Adjust the slider (70% to 90%) to set how much weight Key Persons' preferences carry compared to General Attendees.
   - **Meeting Location / Fixed Restaurant (Optional)**: If you already know the area (e.g., Siam) or have a specific restaurant in mind, enter them.
3. Fill in your availability and preferences then Tap **Create & Vote** to launch the event. Your preferences are saved automatically. A beautiful invitation ticket will be posted to the LINE group.

---

## 📅 Step 2: Submit Availability & Preferences 
**👤 Involved Roles: General Attendees and Key Persons**

Every invited guest must submit their preferences so the suggestion engine can calculate the best fit:

1. Tap **Add Preferences** on the LINE invitation ticket.
2. **RSVP**: Choose **Join** or **Decline**.
3. **Set your availability** (if joining):
   - Tap on the calendar grid to highlight the times you are free.
   - Use the **Bulk Apply** tool to quickly fill weekdays or weekends with a specific time block (e.g. 18:00 - 21:00).
3. **Set your preferences**:
   - **Priorities Selector (Focus Factors)**: Tap the factors (Price, Category, Vibe, Distance, Rating, Parking, Alcohol) in order of preference. The factors you care about most should be ranked at the top.
   - **Budget / Person**: Choose your desired price range.
   - **Preferred Cuisines**: Choose the types of food you want.
   - **Avoid Cuisines**: Flag the foods you cannot or do not want to eat.
   - **Facilities**: Toggle if you require parking space or alcohol service.
   - **Transit Proximity**: Input your departure location and set the maximum walk distance you're comfortable with from the station.
5. Tap **Submit** to log your inputs.

> 💡 The organizer already submitted their preferences in Step 1 — they don't need to vote again.

---

## 🔍 Step 3: Review & Vote on Suggestions 
**👤 Involved Roles: All Attendees**

Once all invited members have submitted their preferences, DISHCISION automatically generates suggestions and sends a **"Suggestions Ready, Let's Vote."** ticket to your LINE chat. Tap **See Suggestions & Vote** to open the dashboard:

1. **Review Suggestions**:
   - **Top Recommended Time Slots**: View the slots ranked by group availability.
   - **Scored Venues**: Browse recommended restaurants with matching badges (e.g. `👑 Key Choice`, `🚇 Transit Friendly`, `💰 Budget Match`).
2. **Check the Scoring Breakdowns**:
   Each restaurant shows detailed scores using two toggle views:
   - **By Factor**: Shows the weighted breakdown score for each focus factor (Price, Cuisine, Vibe, Station, Rating, Parking, Alcohol) relative to the group.
   - **By Attendee**: Shows the individual matching score (out of 100) for each group member for that restaurant.
3. **Submit Your Votes** — Tap **Prefer this time** on each slot you like, and **Prefer** (❤️) or **Veto** (🚫) on restaurants. Then tap **Submit My Votes** to save everything at once. After submitting, you'll see "Waiting for others..."
4. **Wait for the group** — When everyone has submitted, the bot sends a new message: **"All Votes Collected! @organizer"** with a **Review & Decide** button.

---

## 🎫 Step 4: Organizer Decides & Confirms 
**👤 Involved Role: Organizer**

As the Organizer, you are in charge of selecting and confirming the final date, time, and restaurant venue:

1. Tap **Review & Decide** to open the Decision Panel.
2. **Choose Time**:
   - **Pick from list**: Tap a time slot to select it.
   - **Spin Wheel**: Select candidates and spin the interactive wheel.
3. **Choose Restaurant**:
   - **Pick from list**: Tap a restaurant to select it.
   - **Spin Wheel**: Select candidates and spin the wheel.
4. **Lock Decision** — Tap **Confirm & Notify Group**. DISHCISION locks the voting state and sends a **"📍 Finalized Meeting"** ticket to the LINE group with the confirmed date, time, restaurant, and attendee list.

> 👀 Other attendees can open the Decision Panel too — they'll see a read-only view with the live wheel animation and final result.

Enjoy your meal! 🍽️
